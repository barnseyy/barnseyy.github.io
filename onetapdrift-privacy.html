One Tap Drift — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 08/06/2026

Barnseyy built One Tap Drift as a free-to-play mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Gameplay and app data such as scores, leaderboard entries, reward progress, and in-game settings.
  • Optional nickname or player name used for leaderboard display.
  • Device and technical data provided by third-party services, such as device type, operating system, IP address, app diagnostics, advertising identifiers, and ad interaction data.

How We Use Information

  • To run core game features like leaderboards, daily rewards, and saved progress.
  • To improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads and banner ads.
  • To prevent abuse and maintain fair gameplay.

Storage and Processing

  • Some data is stored locally on your device for game functionality.
  • Some data is processed and stored by trusted third-party services to enable game features.

Third-Party Services

One Tap Drift may use third-party services such as:

  • Google AdMob (ad delivery and ad measurement)
  • Google Firebase Realtime Database (leaderboard and reward-related data)

These providers may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising

  • The game may display rewarded and banner advertisements.
  • Ad providers may use device identifiers and similar technologies to deliver and measure ads.

Data Retention

  • We keep data only as long as needed to provide game features and operate the app.
  • You may stop use at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • Local game data may remain on device storage until app data is cleared or the app is removed.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect sensitive personal data from children knowingly. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will review and remove data where appropriate.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can uninstall the app at any time.
  • You can clear local app data from device settings.
  • You can contact us for privacy-related requests.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have any questions, contact barnseyyuk@gmail.com.
