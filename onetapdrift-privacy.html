One Tap Drift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version

One Tap Drift has no account or sign-up. It shows ads and has an online leaderboard, both of which involve some data leaving your device — what that is, and who it goes to, is explained below.

No accounts

There's no sign-up, login, or user account of any kind. Any name you enter is just a nickname you choose for the leaderboard — it isn't verified and isn't linked to your real identity.

Leaderboard

One Tap Drift has an online leaderboard, run on Google Firebase. To appear on it, the app generates a random, anonymous player ID on your device (not tied to your name, email, or any account) and submits it along with your chosen nickname, score, laps, and speed. That's the only gameplay data sent off your device, and it exists solely to show and rank leaderboard entries. Progress, coins, unlocked extras, and settings are otherwise stored locally on your device.

Advertising

One Tap Drift shows ads through Google AdMob — banner ads, occasional interstitials, and optional "watch to earn a reward" video ads. AdMob and its partners may collect device and advertising identifiers and other technical data to serve and measure ads, including to personalise them where permitted in your region. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and, on iOS, is subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt — you can decline tracking there, or turn off ad personalisation for your device in your Google account settings. Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops forced banner and interstitial ads (optional reward-video ads remain available if you choose to watch one).

In-app purchases

Coin packs, "Remove Ads," and the Supporter Pack are all handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play billing. Payment details are processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies — One Tap Drift never sees or stores your card details, only whether a purchase went through.

Other permissions

The app may use haptics (vibration) for feedback and the share sheet if you choose to share a screenshot or result — neither sends any data to Barnsey or to a third party beyond what you explicitly choose to share.

Children's privacy

One Tap Drift is not directed at children under 13. If ads are shown to a general audience, they're not knowingly personalised for under-13 users, in line with Google AdMob's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
